Sustainability in Tourism and the Customer Experience

An introductory training course for tour guides & travel agents

A four-section online course exploring how sustainable tourism practices directly improve the customer experience — and what that means for you in practice. Grounded in the latest research and industry data, with real-world examples throughout.

What you will learn

Four sections.
Practical from the start.

01

Section 1  ·  60 minutes

Introduction to Sustainable Tourism

What sustainable tourism actually means, why it matters, and how it connects to your role in the field.

  • Definitions and global frameworks (GSTC, UN Tourism)
  • The three pillars: environment, economy, community
  • Current trends and traveller expectations
  • Your role as a guide or agent
02

Section 2  ·  60 minutes

Customer Service in Tourism

The foundations of excellent customer service — and the common pitfalls that even experienced professionals fall into.

  • What travellers expect in 2025 and beyond
  • Common customer service failures and how to avoid them
  • Communication styles and adapting to your audience
  • Handling complaints and difficult situations
03

Section 3  ·  60 minutes

Where Sustainability Meets Experience

How sustainable practices directly improve the customer experience — through specific touch points you encounter every day.

  • Environmental touch points and guest perception
  • Local destination experience and authenticity
  • Community involvement as a service differentiator
  • Communicating sustainability without preaching
04

Section 4  ·  60 minutes

Practical Application

Real-world scenarios and worked examples — how to put what you've learned into action with actual guests and clients.

  • Addressing each touch point in practice
  • Scripts and language for common situations
  • Building sustainable practice into your daily work
  • Measuring and improving your own performance
